Project Overview

This project presents an exploratory view of the electric vehicle market using Tableau visualizations. It brings together different views of vehicle categories, sales trends, manufacturer market share, regional distribution, vehicle price and range, and battery performance.

The aim is to make market information easier to explore and support data-informed discussion about the EV industry.
