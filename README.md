# EV Market Performance and Insights

## Project Overview

EV Market Performance and Insights is a data visualization project designed to explore electric vehicle (EV) market trends, sales performance, manufacturer market share, battery performance, driving range, and regional opportunities.

The project uses Tableau dashboards and visualizations to present market information in an understandable format and help stakeholders explore patterns in the electric vehicle industry.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze EV sales across different vehicle categories.
- Explore manufacturer market share and regional distribution.
- Understand sales trends over different model years.
- Compare vehicle price, driving range, and battery performance.
- Examine battery degradation and vehicle performance.
- Identify potential opportunities in the EV market through data visualization.

1. Sales by Category
2. Global EV Map
3. Sales Trend by Year
4. Price versus Range
5. Battery Degradation Matrix
6. Top Models by Horsepower
7. Manufacturer Market Share
